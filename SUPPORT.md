# Support for Mythos Agent

Thank you for using Mythos Agent! Here are the best ways to get help and support:

## Community & Questions

- **GitHub Issues:** For bug reports or feature requests, use our [Issue Templates](https://github.com/Academypeep/0707/issues/new/choose).
- **GitHub Discussions:** For general usage questions, security agent architecture ideas, or integration advice, participate in GitHub Discussions.
- **Documentation:** Explore our guides in `docs/` and `QUICKSTART.md`.

## Security Issues

If you suspect you have identified a vulnerability in Mythos Agent, please refer to our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and report it privately rather than creating a public issue.

## Commercial & Enterprise Support

For custom enterprise deployment, high-throughput swarm scaling, or dedicated support agreements, contact the maintainers at `kaype8996@gmail.com` or sponsor the project through our corporate tiers on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/Academypeep).
